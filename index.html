THE FOUNDING OF A HOME

HOW OAKHAVEN BECAME

Before the roads, shops, and walls, this place was only wilderness. Step through the moments that turned a lonely clearing into the town at the heart of Valvondor.

CHAPTER I

Before Oakhaven

Long before anyone called it home, the land was untamed woodland. Travelers passed through, but few stayed. The forest provided shelter, timber, and food — if you survived long enough to use them.

CHAPTER II

The First Fires

A handful of settlers chose the clearing as a place to stop running. They raised rough shelters, cut the first paths through the trees, and kept a fire burning through the night. That small camp became the beginning of Oakhaven.

CHAPTER III

Brimvault

When rich stone and ore were found beneath the nearby hills, everything changed. Miners came. Traders followed. What had been a camp became a village, and Oakhaven finally had something worth protecting.

CHAPTER IV

A Town Takes Shape

The blacksmith fired his forge. The general store opened its doors. Homes crowded the growing roads, fences marked safe ground, and Oakhaven became a true town rather than a place travelers simply passed through.

CHAPTER V

Oakhaven Today

The town survived hard winters, dangerous forests, and goblin attacks from the west. Now it stands as the first safe home for a new generation of adventurers. The town's history is written. Yours is about to begin.
